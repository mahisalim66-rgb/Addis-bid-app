# Privacy Policy for Addis Bid (አዲስ ቢድ)

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to **Addis Bid** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you provide when using our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you use Addis Bid, we may collect the following information:
- **Account & Identity Information**: Your full name, phone number, and account username.
- **Transaction & Payment Verification**: Information related to Telebirr deposit references and transaction SMS screenshots/codes submitted for manual admin verification to fund your wallet.
- **Bidding & Activity**: Logs of bids placed and auctions participated in.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely to:
- Create and manage your Addis Bid account.
- Authenticate and secure user sessions.
- Verify Telebirr deposits and update your wallet balance.
- Process winning notifications and facilitate giveaway prize deliveries.
- Improve application performance and user experience.

---

### 3. Data Protection and Security
We implement strict industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third parties.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
Our app integrates with:
- **Telebirr** (Ethio Telecom payment service): Used for financial transfers. We do not store your bank PINs or private credentials.
- **Firebase / Google Cloud Services**: Used for data storage and backend logic.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our services are intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years old.
